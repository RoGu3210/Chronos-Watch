An improbable
forest in stone.

Begun in 1882, the Basílica i Temple Expiatori de la Sagrada Família has been under continuous construction for over 140 years. Gaudí designed eighteen spires — twelve for the apostles, four for the evangelists, one for Mary, one for Jesus — set above a triple façade that catalogues the gospel in stone. Completion is now projected for 2026.
